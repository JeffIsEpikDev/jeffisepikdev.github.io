Why we built it

Finding people shouldn't take ten texts

wya started with a simple problem. When a group is supposed to meet up, it's hard to tell where everyone is, who's actually coming, and who's still at home. So we built a live view of your people. It makes plans feel real and in the moment, and it helps you spend more time around the people you care about.
